Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Pure Fitness · Miicon Solutions

1. Who we are

The Pure Fitness application is governed and developed by Miicon Solutions (“Miicon”, “we”, “us”). Pure Fitness is the membership gym brand and mobile app offered in Bangladesh under that application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Related commitments appear in our Terms & Conditions.

2. Information we collect

  • Account & contact. Phone number (E.164), name, emergency contact details, blood group (if you provide it), and optional profile photo.
  • Sign-in. OTP verification codes sent by SMS; if you use Google sign-in, we receive an identity token and associated email/name from Google/Firebase as configured.
  • Membership & payments. Plan selection, membership status and dates (including expiry used for renewals and re-admission rules), payment amounts, discounts/offers, payment records, and transaction references from gateways or reception cash intake.
  • Facility access. Check-in / attendance history and, where used, biometric templates enrolled on facility devices for entry verification.
  • Device & app. Basic technical data needed to run the service (for example app version, crash diagnostics if enabled, and language preference).
  • Staff data. Staff phone numbers, display names, and authenticator (TOTP) enrollment status for facility administration.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure your membership account (including OTP and optional Google linking).
  • Provide gym access, renewals, plan changes, admission/re-admission pricing, offers, and payment history in the app.
  • Communicate about membership, security (OTP), and support requests.
  • Operate reception workflows (for example linking a new phone after a lost-device visit).
  • Improve safety, prevent fraud, and maintain facility operations.
  • Comply with legal obligations applicable in Bangladesh.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers that help us send SMS OTPs, process payments, host the API, or provide authentication infrastructure (such as Firebase for Google sign-in).
  • Facility staff who need membership details to run the gym (roster, cash reception, fingerprint enrollment support).
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Biometrics

Fingerprint or other biometric data used for gym entry is collected for access control only. It is processed on facility systems under staff procedures and is not used for unrelated marketing. Ask reception if you need help re-enrolling or deactivating biometric access for your membership.

6. Retention

We keep account, membership, and payment records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting requirements. Biometric templates are retained while your membership requires facility biometric entry, and are removed or deactivated according to facility procedure when no longer needed.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to a membership API (encrypted transport, hashed OTP/refresh tokens, encrypted TOTP secrets for staff, and access controls). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; please protect your phone and do not share OTP codes.

8. Your choices

  • Update profile details in the app where editing is available.
  • Request phone changes through the in-app flow or reception (lost phone).
  • Disconnect optional Google linking where the product supports it, subject to account recovery rules.
  • Contact Pure Fitness to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable under local law and operational constraints (for example active membership or payment records).

9. Children

The app is intended for members who can lawfully hold a gym membership. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children for online accounts without appropriate guardian involvement where required.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Significant changes may also be highlighted in the app or at the facility.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: visit Pure Fitness reception or use the support channels published in the app.
