Pure Fitness pricing has a clear structure. Exact taka amounts change when plans or offers are updated; always rely on the checkout or reception preview for the amount you will pay.

  • Renewal. Extending the same plan charges the period fee only — no admission — while your membership is active, or within 180 days after the paid membership end date.
  • Plan change. Switching to a different duration or plan uses that plan’s period/renewal pricing shown at checkout. No re-admission while you are eligible under the rules above.
  • Re-admission. If your membership has been expired for 180 calendar days or more after the end date that was set by a successful payment, the next purchase includes the admission fee again (unless a live offer waives it). The 180-day count starts from that payment-backed expiry — not from registration.

Limited “first-N” seats may apply; when seats are gone or the offer dates end, standard pricing returns. Claiming checkout can reserve a limited seat before payment is confirmed; cancelled or failed payments release that seat.
  • Payment channels. Online payments may go through a third-party gateway. Cash may be accepted at reception when offered. Failed or cancelled payments do not activate or extend membership.
